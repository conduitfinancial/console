Archiving retires the saved for payouts capability: the contact leaves every payout picker, and it leaves this list unless it is also whitelisted — in which case the row stays, carrying its Conduit registration alone. It is not a delete: the record stays behind the payouts that named it, and there is no way back — save the destination again from a payout form if you need it. Revoking is Conduit's side: the destination can no longer be named on a payout, and getting it back means registering it again, which Conduit reviews from the start. Neither one does the other's job — a revoked contact is still saved for payouts, and an archived one may still be whitelisted.
