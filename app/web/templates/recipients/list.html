Customer {{ customer_id }}. Registering a destination with Conduit is what gives a contact its registered for intercompany payouts capability: an intercompany payout to a bank account is refused without one, and only registered destinations can be named on a payout.

What registering doesn't cover

It is not what a transfer between two Conduit accounts needs — that arm names the destination account directly and gates on nothing. Conduit reviews every registration first. The customer's existing destinations are on their Contacts page, which is also where a registration's status is explained.
